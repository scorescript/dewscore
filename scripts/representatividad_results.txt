The spatial representativeness of the assigned AEMET stations was assessed by computing, for each of the 21 municipalities in the study area, the great-circle distance between the station and the municipal centroid, the difference in elevation between the station and the mean municipal altitude, and the number of active AEMET stations within a 30 km radius. The mean station-to-centroid distance was 4.9 km (range: 0.1--10.4 km), and the mean elevation difference was 257 m (range: 10--1252 m). On average, 21.5 AEMET stations were available within 30 km of each municipal centroid (range: 15--27). Municipalities with the largest elevation differences (e.g., Tejeda and Artenara, located above 1000 m a.s.l.) were assigned to the nearest available high-altitude stations, minimising the lapse-rate bias in dew point derivation.
